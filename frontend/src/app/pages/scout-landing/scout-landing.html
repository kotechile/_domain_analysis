FAQ

What people need to know before they start.

Is Scout a domain marketplace?

No. Scout helps you discover and evaluate domains listed across auction sources. It is a research and decision platform, not the seller of record.

What does Scout analyze?

Scout brings together SEO metrics, backlinks, keyword data, traffic indicators, archive history, and AI-assisted notes to help you review domain opportunities more efficiently.

Who should use Scout?

Domain investors, SEO operators, agencies, and builders who want a faster, more repeatable due diligence workflow.
