FAQ

Pricing questions, answered.

What counts as a credit?

One credit is consumed each time you run a full domain analysis. Browsing the marketplace and viewing basic scores are free on all plans.

Do credits expire?

Credits from monthly subscriptions reset each billing cycle. Credits purchased as one-time packs do not expire.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing page. When you upgrade, the new credits are available immediately.

What happens if I run out of credits?

You can buy credit packs or upgrade your plan at any time. You'll always have access to free-tier marketplace browsing.

Is there a refund policy?

Credits are allocated for analysis compute and are non-refundable after use. Unused subscription credits roll over if you stay on the same plan.
